Sports in the school

Bahati House (Wellstar Bright S.S.)

  • Bahati House is one of the prominent student houses at Wellstar Bright Secondary School.
  • It is especially known for its strong performance in sports competitions.
  • The house made history by winning the inter-house sports competition three consecutive times, showing consistency, teamwork, and discipline.
  • Members of Bahati House are often recognized for:
    • High levels of team spirit
    • Strong athletic ability
    • Good organization and leadership during competitions
  • Their success has made Bahati House a dominant and respected house within the school.

Other Houses at Wellstar Bright S.S.

Kikungwe House

  • Kikungwe House is known for its resilience and determination.
  • Although it may not always take the top position, it consistently competes strongly in sports and academics.
  • The house is often praised for its teamwork and unity.

Ssendawula House

  • Ssendawula House has a reputation for discipline and hard work.
  • It performs well in both co-curricular activities and academics.
  • Members are known for their commitment and organization, making them a strong competitor.

Kiwanuka House

  • Kiwanuka House is recognized for its enthusiasm and active participation in school events.
  • It often stands out in cultural and social activities, in addition to sports.
  • The house promotes creativity, cooperation, and school spirit.

Conclusion

  • While Bahati House has recently dominated sports with three consecutive victories, the other houses—Kikungwe, Ssendawula, and Kiwanuka—each bring unique strengths.
  • Together, they create a healthy competitive environment that encourages participation, excellence, and unity across the school.

MDD Competitions at Wellstar Bright S.S.

Overview

  • MDD (Music, Dance and Drama) is an important co-curricular activity at the school.
  • Students from different houses such as Bahati, Kikungwe, Ssendawula, and Kiwanuka actively participate.

Activities Involved

  • Traditional dances (Baganda, Acholi, Banyankole, etc.)
  • School choir performances
  • Drama and plays with messages about society (e.g., discipline, education, health)
  • Use of drums, costumes, and props

During Competitions

  • Students perform on a school stage or assembly ground
  • Judges assess:
    • Creativity
    • Coordination
    • Costume and presentation
  • Houses compete, creating excitement and school spirit

MDD Pictures (What is Captured)

Common scenes in pictures:

  • Students in colorful traditional wear
  • Groups dancing in formation
  • Drummers and singers performing
  • Actors on stage during drama
